BirrX privacy policy

Publisher: Tameru · Effective date: 21 November 2026

BirrX is a personal-finance tracker. Contact Tameru at rhematube209@gmail.com for privacy questions.

Accounts and sign-in

Supabase processes your email, authentication credentials and any profile name submitted during registration. Internet access is required when opening BirrX. Financial records are not automatically backed up or synchronized to a cloud account.

Financial records and SMS

Transactions, account masks, reported balances, budgets, goals, notes, category rules, preferences and review decisions are stored locally on your device. Optional Android SMS access reads messages from supported financial senders to identify transactions and package updates. BirrX does not send SMS or read personal contacts. You can instead use manual entry, CSV or pasted messages.

Message text is processed transiently and is not stored as raw SMS in the financial database. Unmatched review records retain sender, timestamp, classification and deduplication metadata. Receipt links provide reference identifiers; scanning does not open those links. Receipt images are not persistently stored by default.

Optional AI analysis

Only after your separate confirmation, selected unmatched messages are re-read and masked, with full phone/account numbers and links removed before transmission through Supabase to Google Gemini. Names, dates, amounts and remaining financial context may still be included. AI results require your review before saving. The BirrX backend stores usage counters and does not intentionally store message bodies or AI responses. Google and Supabase process transmitted data under their applicable terms and retention practices; do not assume provider processing has zero retention.

Notifications and device security

Android handles permission requests, reminders and notification delivery. Notification history is maintained locally and older daily inbox items are removed. Hide amounts controls financial figures in transaction alerts. Device app lock uses Android authentication; BirrX does not receive your fingerprint or device PIN. App lock does not encrypt exported backups.

Exports and retention

Backups and CSV exports may contain readable financial information. You choose where to save or share them; BirrX cannot erase copies already exported or shared. Local records remain until you delete them or clear the installation's app data. Raw messages remain in your phone's SMS inbox and are controlled by your messaging app.

Account information remains until account deletion is processed. Per-user AI usage counters are removed as part of verified account deletion. Global usage aggregates contain no account identity. Older quota rows are pruned during service use; inactivity alone does not guarantee immediate removal. Service providers may retain operational records under their own terms and applicable obligations.

Advertising

This version does not integrate AdMob or request advertising. Financial SMS and SMS-derived data are not used for advertising. Advertising integration would require an updated policy and applicable privacy controls.

Your choices and deletion

You may revoke SMS, notification and camera permissions in device settings, use manual import, change appearance, export your data, and request account deletion. Deleting a server account does not remotely erase other devices, original SMS, or exported files. Clear BirrX app storage on each device to remove local records, after saving any backup you wish to retain.

Contact the support address for access, correction or deletion requests. This policy may be updated when BirrX's processing changes.
