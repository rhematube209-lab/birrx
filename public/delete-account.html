Delete online without the app

Sign in to delete your account

The secure deletion flow requires your current password and typed confirmation. If the deletion service is unavailable, your device data is not removed; use the email alternative below.

Email alternative

Send a request from your registered email address to rhematube209@gmail.com with the subject BirrX account deletion request. If you no longer have access to that address, contact support to discuss identity verification. Do not send your password, OTP, banking SMS or financial statement.

Prepare deletion request email

This opens an email draft. You must send it. Opening this page does not delete your account or submit a request.

What happens next

Support verifies that you own the account before deleting its authentication profile and associated per-user AI usage counters. Support confirms completion or explains any information that must be retained and why. There is no automatic completion promise when you open the email draft.

Remove device data separately

The updated in-app deletion flow removes only the selected account’s local workspace. Clearing all Android app storage or browser site data removes every BirrX account saved on that device; do not do that if you need to keep another account’s records.

Financial records are stored locally. After exporting any backup you want to keep, use Android Settings → Apps → BirrX → Storage → Clear data (wording varies by phone). For the website, clear BirrX's site storage in your browser. Do this on each device you used. This removes local records and preferences; it does not delete original SMS or exported/shared backups.

You can also start the email request from Profile → BirrX account → Delete account.
